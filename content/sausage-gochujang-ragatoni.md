- ⏲️ Prep time: 30 min
- 🍳 Cook time: 20 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 4

## Ingredients

- 1 shallot
- 3 garlic cloves
- 6 pork sausages
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 220 ml single cream
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 limes
- 40g parmesan
- 400g rigatnoi
- 30g fresh coriander
- 15g panko breadcrumbs


## Directions

1. Mince shallot and garlic.

2. Heat olive oil in pan. Add sausages. Fry until browned. Add splash of water to deglaze and remove meat.

3. Add butter to pan. Once melterd add minced shallot and garlic. Fry until softened.

4. Add gochujang, cream, honey, and juice of limes. Grate in 3/4 of parmesan. Add back sauage meat. Mix and season with salt and pepper. Leave to simmer.

5. Boil water and cook pasta. Save some pasta water for sauce.

6. While pasta cooks, finely chop coriander. Fry breadcrumbs in seperate pan with glug of olive oil until golden. Grate in remaining parmesan and some coriander.

7. Add pasat water to sauce and mix with remaining coriander.

8. Add pasta to sauce and serve.
